Support

Request a GR Music refund

GR Music is operated by GR Studios AI. You can request billing help without installing the app or signing in here.

Email a refund request

Contact grstudiosai@grstudios.shop. Include your GR Music account email, receipt or transaction reference, purchase date, product and the issue. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or full payment-card numbers.

The button opens your email application. The request is sent only after you send the email, and submitting it does not automatically approve a refund or cancel a subscription.

If Google Play processed the charge, you can also use Google Play’s refund guidance. For website purchases, contact GR Music support with the Stripe receipt reference.
