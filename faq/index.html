Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about the studio, its creative projects, software, and this website.

What is GR Studios AI LLC?

An independent studio developing music, comics, gaming projects, artwork, and software.

Who is ZPHRIS?

ZPHRIS (pronounced Zephyris) is the studio’s music artist identity.

What is ShadowPulse?

ShadowPulse is a cross-platform gaming team and connected creative brand, including original music and comics.

What is GR Catgirl?

GR Catgirl is the studio’s AI assistant project, separate from the company name.

Can I download GR software here?

Use the Downloads page to check available resources. A project being listed does not mean a public installer has been released.

Where can I see release information?

Visit Releases, Music, and News for the information published on this site.

Is the website an account portal?

No. This version of the public site does not provide account sign-in or a file-upload portal.

Can I reuse the artwork or music?

Do not assume material is free to reuse. Review the studio’s terms and request permission where needed.

Where are the privacy policy and terms?

Visit Privacy and Terms. The studio’s legal site is legal.grstudios.shop.

How can I get support?

Start with the Support page for the appropriate project and information.
