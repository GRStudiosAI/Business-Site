Privacy Policy for Discord Bots

GR Studios AI LLC — official policy text adapted from the supplied legal source file.

Source: DiscordPrivacy.tsx · Version: 1.0.0-DS

Terms of Service · Privacy Policy · Refund Policy · DMCA Policy · Discord Bot Privacy · Discord Bot Terms

This Privacy Policy applies to all Discord bots and related services operated through this website (collectively, "our bots"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your data is handled securely and transparently.

By adding our bots to your Discord server or interacting with them, you agree to the data practices described in this policy.

Data We Collect

Our bots collect and process only the minimum data necessary to provide their features and maintain security. This may include:

  • Discord User IDs: To identify users and associate settings or permissions.
  • Discord Server (Guild) IDs: To identify servers and store per-server configurations.
  • Channel IDs: To determine where commands are executed or where to send messages.
  • Message IDs: When required for specific bot features such as reaction roles or moderation logs.
  • Slash Commands and Interactions: Data about the commands you run and how you interact with bot components.
  • Server Configuration Settings: Any custom settings or preferences you configure within the bot.
  • Technical Logs: Information necessary for debugging, security auditing, or abuse prevention.

How We Use Data

The data we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain bot functionality.
  • To store and manage individual server settings and preferences.
  • To improve the reliability and performance of our services.
  • To prevent abuse, spam, and unauthorized access.
  • To comply with legal obligations or respond to valid legal requests.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Your privacy is important to us. We adhere to the following principles regarding your data:

  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties.
  • Limited Sharing: We do not share data with third parties except when necessary to operate the service (e.g., hosting providers) or when required by law.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain data only for as long as it is necessary to provide the services requested or as required for security and legal purposes.

Data may be deleted when:

  • The bot is removed from a server (automated cleanup of server-specific data).
  • A user or server administrator requests data deletion.
  • The data is no longer needed for any of the purposes listed above.

Security Safeguards

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect the information we store. This includes access controls, encryption where appropriate, and regular security reviews.

However, please be aware that no system can guarantee absolute security. We cannot guarantee the 100% security of any information transmitted over the internet or Discord.

Third-Party Interactions

Our bots operate through the Discord platform. Use of our bots is also subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

In some cases, our bots may interact with other third-party services (for example, to fetch external data). Each of these services has its own privacy practices, which we do not control.

User Rights

Users may request information about the data we have stored related to their Discord account or server. You may also request the deletion of such data where applicable.
